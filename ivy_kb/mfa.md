# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA uses an authenticator app registered to your account. It is required for password reset, VPN, and SSO sign-in.

## Lost or replaced MFA device
If you no longer have access to your authenticator app (new phone, lost device, app deleted), you **cannot** self-serve. Contact the IT desk to re-enroll MFA after identity verification. Do not share codes with anyone.
