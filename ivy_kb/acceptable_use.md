# Acceptable Use & Scope of IT Support

IT supports **company IT systems and managed devices only**.

- **Personal email/accounts** (e.g., personal Gmail) are not supported on managed work devices.
- **Personal/home devices** (home routers, personal phones, home Wi-Fi) are out of scope; refer users to the manufacturer.
- **Non-IT requests** (weather, travel booking, HR/payroll questions) are out of scope — politely decline and point to the right team.
