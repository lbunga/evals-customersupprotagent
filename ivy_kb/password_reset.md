# Password Reset & Account Lockout

## Self-service password reset
All password resets are handled through the self-service portal at **password.company.com**.
1. Click **Reset Password**.
2. Verify your identity with your registered **MFA** method.
3. Set a new password that meets policy: **12+ characters, at least 1 number, at least 1 symbol**.
The old password is retired immediately.

## Expired passwords
Expired passwords are reset the same way through the self-service portal. Sign in with MFA and choose a new compliant password.

## Account lockout from failed attempts
A standard lockout caused by too many failed sign-in attempts is resolved by self-service: either wait for the lockout window to clear, or reset your password through the portal with MFA.

> Note: Repeated lockouts combined with unusual activity (e.g., emails marked read before you open them) are NOT a routine lockout — see **Security Incidents**.
